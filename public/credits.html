Asset credits

The opening astronaut portrait and retained rear reference were generated with OpenAI imagegen. The traveling astronaut is original volumetric geometry authored for this portfolio. The planetary surface map was generated with OpenAI imagegen and applied to rotating 3D spheres. Stars, nebula and flowing black-hole light render procedurally in real time. No movie frames or movie textures are used.

An earlier prototype used Astronaut by Poly, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0, distributed by Google’s model-viewer project. The model is retained as an unused reference; its texture was resized and recompressed. The original creator does not endorse this portfolio.
